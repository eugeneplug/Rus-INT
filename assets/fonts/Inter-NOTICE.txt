Inter web fonts prepared from the user-provided Inter.zip.

Sources:
  Inter-VariableFont_opsz,wght.ttf
  Inter-Italic-VariableFont_opsz,wght.ttf

Converted to WOFF2 with FontTools 4.68.0 and Brotli 1.2.1.
Split into disjoint Cyrillic, Latin and extended Unicode subsets.
All source Unicode characters and OpenType layout features are retained across the subsets.
Variable axes retained: wght 100-900 and opsz 14-32.
The Cyrillic and Latin normal subsets are preloaded; other subsets and italics load when used.
